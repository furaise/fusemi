WHAT WE DO

Semiconductor backend services

From architecture to sign-off, FuSemi supports reliable, high-performance chip design.

Design for Test

DFT architecture using scan, MBIST and LBIST methodologies to improve test coverage, cost efficiency, and product quality.

Synthesis

RTL-to-gate implementation optimized for performance, power, area, and multi-mode multi-corner design constraints.

Static Timing Analysis

Timing analysis and closure to identify setup and hold issues and verify reliable operation across conditions.

Physical Design

Backend implementation services focused on predictable performance, robust layouts, and manufacturable results.

Physical Verification

Verification support that helps designs meet physical and manufacturing requirements.

Formal Verification

Rigorous verification approaches for dependable designs and engineering change management.
